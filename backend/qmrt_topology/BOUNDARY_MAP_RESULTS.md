# PARAMETER BOUNDARY MAP: Results

## Key Finding

**The QMRT dynamical medium is in the STRONG spacetime coupling regime across the entire tested parameter range.**

| Dimension | α Range | I_TS Range | ρ(O,S) Range | Regime |
|-----------|---------|------------|--------------|--------|
| 2D | 0.05 - 0.95 | 0.84 - 0.88 | -0.999 to -0.990 | **STRONG** |
| 3D | 0.05 - 0.95 | 0.79 - 0.86 | -1.000 to -0.992 | **STRONG** |

## Interpretation

### What This Means

1. **No phase transitions in α**
   - The system does not pass through weak → transition → strong phases
   - Spacetime coupling is already "on" even at α = 0.05
   - The backreaction parameter α modulates *strength*, not *existence*

2. **Spacetime emergence is robust**
   - The coupling I_TS > 0.6 everywhere (strong threshold)
   - The O-S correlation |ρ| > 0.99 everywhere (near-perfect)
   - This is not a fragile phenomenon — it's structurally stable

3. **3D has stronger ordering constraint**
   - ρ(O,S) is closer to -1.00 in 3D than 2D
   - 3D geometry more strongly constrains causal ordering
   - This makes physical sense: more directions = more constraints

### What This Does NOT Mean

- It does NOT mean there are no transitions at all
- Transitions may exist at:
  - Very low α < 0.05 (below our test range)
  - Different λ or γ values
  - Different initial conditions
  - Finite-size boundaries

## Physical Interpretation

### The Correct Statement

> The backreaction parameter α controls the **intensity** of spacetime emergence, not its **existence**. Within the tested range (α ∈ [0.05, 0.95]), the system is always in a regime where:
> - Temporal-spatial coupling forms (I_TS > 0.6)
> - Ordering is constrained by geometry (|ρ(O,S)| > 0.99)
> - The medium self-balances (E_cv < 0.05)

### What α Actually Does

As α increases:
- S increases (more spatial structure)
- I_TS increases slightly (0.84 → 0.88 in 2D)
- |ρ(O,S)| decreases slightly (0.9999 → 0.990)

This suggests:
- Higher α = stronger coupling
- But even low α has strong coupling
- The system is "always on" in this regime

## Next Steps

To find actual transitions, need to explore:

1. **Extended α range**: α < 0.05 or α > 1.0
2. **λ (relaxation) sweep**: How does medium response time affect transitions?

## Conclusion

The QMRT dynamical medium exhibits **universal strong coupling** across the tested backreaction range. Spacetime emergence is not a fragile phase transition but a robust structural property of the system.

This is actually a strong result: it means the framework doesn't require fine-tuning to achieve emergent spacetime.
