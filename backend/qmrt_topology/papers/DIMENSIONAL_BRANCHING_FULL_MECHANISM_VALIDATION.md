# Dimensional Branching: Full-Mechanism Validation

**Date**: December 2025  
**Status**: VALIDATED — Dimensional Transition Survives

---

## Executive Summary

The dimensional branching phenomenon **survives** under the full QMRT mechanism with τ self-regulation. During sustained driving, both full and reduced models show nearly identical behavior.

The key difference only appears post-cutoff:
- **Full mechanism**: Self-limiting decay
- **Reduced model**: Sustained high population (momentum artifact)

---

## Comparison Results

### During Sustained Driving

| Interval | Full Pop | Full Dim | Reduced Pop | Reduced Dim |
|----------|----------|----------|-------------|-------------|
| 50 | 520 | 1.46 | 501 | 1.49 |
| 100 | 905 | 1.72 | 851 | 1.72 |
| 200 | 788 | 1.69 | 730 | 1.74 |
| 500 | 409 | 1.57 | 364 | 1.60 |

**Observation**: Populations and dimensions are **nearly identical** during driving.

### Sweet-Spot Effect Confirmed

Both models show a non-monotonic relationship:
- Interval 100 achieves highest dimension (~1.72)
- Both stronger (50) and weaker (500) driving give lower dimension
- This is NOT a reduced-model artifact

### What This Means

1. **Phase 6-9 driven-scaffold results are likely valid**
   - The τ self-regulation doesn't significantly affect behavior during driving
   - Dimensional structure emerges similarly in both models

2. **The "endogenous regeneration" effect was correctly identified as reduced-model-specific**
   - Post-cutoff behavior differs dramatically
   - Full mechanism shows self-limiting decay

3. **Dimensional branching is a robust QMRT feature**
   - Not an artifact of missing mechanism
   - Survives under τ self-regulation
