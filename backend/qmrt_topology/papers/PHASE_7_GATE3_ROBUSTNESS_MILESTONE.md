## Analysis

### 1. No Dimension Transition

- Dimension range across all tests: 1.10 → 1.21 (span = 0.11)
- Even the most extreme forcing (triple extreme) only raises d by 0.11
- **The ~1D filamentary structure is robust to extreme combined forcing**

### 2. Structural Enrichment Without Dimension Change

- Triangle count increases dramatically (7K → 54K)
- Average degree doubles (12 → 26)
- But clustering coefficient *decreases* slightly (dilution effect)
- **Local structure becomes richer while global topology stays filamentary**

### 3. Decoupling of Local and Global Organization

| Scale | Effect of Extreme Forcing |
|-------|---------------------------|
| Local (triangles, degree) | Enriched |
| Global (dimension) | Unchanged |

This suggests **independent degrees of freedom** at different scales.
