## Program Arc

This document summarizes the five-paper progression of the QMRT (Quark Medium Relativity Theory) research program, investigating whether organized structures and spacetime-like properties can emerge from continuous scalar field dynamics.

**Complete progression**: Formation → Persistence → Localization → Causal Control → Population Ecology

---

## Paper 1: Formation

**Title**: Statistical Lifecycle Rules for Coherent Structure Formation

**Core finding**: Coherent structures in scalar fields form according to measurable statistical rules—nucleation, growth, persistence, and decay follow quantifiable distributions rather than random fluctuations.

**Significance**: Establishes that organization is not arbitrary; it has reproducible statistics.

---

## Paper 2: Sustained Organization

**Title**: Energy Requirements for Sustained Organization

**Core finding**: Sustained organization requires continuous energy input. Without driving, structures decay toward equilibrium and organization dissipates.

**Significance**: Establishes that persistent organization is not free; it requires maintained conditions.

---

## Paper 3: Organization Localization

**Title**: β-Asymmetry and Spatial Localization of Organization

**Core finding**: Spatially varying β-coupling can localize organization to preferred regions. However, the resulting structures do not exhibit matter-like interactions—they remain field excitations without conserved identity.

**Significance**: Establishes that spatial control of organization is possible, but does not automatically produce particle-like behavior.

---

## Paper 4: Causal Attractor Control of Topological Populations

**Title**: Causal Attractor Control of Topological Defect Populations in a Topological-Memory Medium

**Core finding**: Spatially varying channel coupling creates a causal attractor landscape for topological defects. The coupling gradient regulates where defects regenerate, drift, and survive. Inverting the gradient reverses all three effects.

**Key evidence**:
- 64% of births in high-coupling region (vs 44% uniform)
- Mean drift toward high coupling
- Lifetime 146 steps in high-coupling vs 56 in low-coupling
- Inverted gradient reverses all effects

**Significance**: First successful co-alignment of topological memory with spatial control. Demonstrates causal (not merely correlational) regulation of defect populations.

**3D Validation**: Successfully extended to 3D with 11.44× interior/periphery density ratio and confirmed gradient inversion reversal.

---

## Paper 5: Population Ecology Under Attractor Control

**Title**: Population Ecology of Topological Defects in an Active Selective Medium

**Core finding**: Within the 3D causal attractor regime, topological defect populations exhibit structured population-level behavior characterized by regional niche differentiation, finite carrying capacity, and non-equilibrium steady-state dynamics.

**Key evidence**:
- **Niche differentiation**: Interior 3.5–5.4× denser, 3× lower turnover, 2.7× tighter spacing
- **NESS dynamics**: Statistical stationarity with episodic bursts, ~14-step relaxation
- **Carrying capacity**: Interior saturates at ~31 defects; excess spills to periphery

**Significance**: Establishes that the medium functions as an **active selective environment**—maintaining, filtering, and spatially organizing topological populations through selection rather than force modification.

**Boundaries**: Population-ecology analog only. No biological life, heredity, metabolism, or matter-like bound states claimed.

## What Has Been Established

1. **Formation is statistical, not random** — coherent structures emerge with quantifiable lifecycle rules
2. **Persistence requires driving** — organization decays without sustained energy input
3. **Localization is achievable** — spatial coupling can bias where organization appears
4. **Topological populations can be causally controlled** — coupling gradients act as attractors
5. **3D validation** — causal attractor mechanism survives dimensional extension
6. **Selection mechanism** — attractor controls population, not interaction law
7. **Population ecology** — niche differentiation, carrying capacity, NESS dynamics

---

## What Has NOT Been Established

- Matter-like bound-state behavior
- Conserved defect populations (statistical only)
- Biological life, heredity, metabolism
- Open-ended evolution or adaptation
- Direct connection to physical spacetime emergence
