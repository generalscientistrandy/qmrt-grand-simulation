## Paper 4 Summary

**Title**: Causal Attractor Control of Topological Defect Populations in a Topological-Memory Medium

**Core claim**: Spatially varying channel coupling creates a causal attractor landscape for topological defects, regulating where defects regenerate, drift, and survive.

**Decisive evidence**: Inverting the coupling gradient reverses both transport direction and population localization.
