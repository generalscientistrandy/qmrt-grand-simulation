## Theoretical Interpretation

### Balance Without Attraction

The ~50/50 balance found in Phase 11f is NOT maintained by:
- Attraction between opposite signs
- Repulsion between same signs
- Any preferential spatial arrangement

Instead, balance appears to be:
- **Statistical**: Equal creation rates of + and - defects
- **Non-interacting**: Sectors evolve independently
- **Compensating**: Fluctuations cancel on average

The medium creates and destroys + and - defects at equal rates, but:
- Defects don't "know" each other's sign
- There's no attractive or repulsive force between opposite signs
- Balance is a global statistical property, not a local interaction law
