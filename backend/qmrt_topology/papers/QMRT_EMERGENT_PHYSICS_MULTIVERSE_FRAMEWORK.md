## 1. Introduction: Beyond Substance-Based Dark Sector

Standard cosmology treats dark matter and dark energy as **substances** — particles (WIMPs, axions) for dark matter, a cosmological constant or quintessence field for dark energy. The QMRT framework offers a fundamentally different interpretation.

**Core Thesis**:
> Dark matter and dark energy are not substances. They are **observational signatures of branch alignment differences** in the quark medium. Physics itself is emergent from initial conditions, and what we call "dark matter halos" are patterns specific to our universe's starting state.

## 3. Branch Alignment and Dark Phenomena

### 3.1 The Branch Structure

The quark medium consists of **branches** — local regions with specific oscillation properties. Globally, these branches are balanced (the medium maintains overall equilibrium). Locally, they can be misaligned.

| Scale | Branch State | Observable Effect |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|
| Universal (medium-wide) | Balanced | No net anomaly |
| Local (around matter) | Misaligned | Dark matter signatures |
| Transitional (expansion) | Activating | Dark energy signatures |

### 3.2 Dark Matter as Branch Misalignment

**Standard view**: Dark matter is invisible particles creating extra gravity.

**QMRT view**: Dark matter is the **local misalignment of medium branches** around visible matter concentrations.

When matter concentrates:
1. Local branches become differentially stressed
2. τ (branch property) varies spatially
3. Observers perceive this as "extra gravitational influence"
4. They attribute it to hidden mass — but it's branch structure

**Why halos?** Because branch misalignment follows specific patterns based on:
- The geometry of matter concentration
- Our universe's starting conditions
- The local-vs-global balance dynamics

### 3.3 Dark Energy as Branch Activation

**Standard view**: Dark energy is a repulsive cosmological constant.

**QMRT view**: Dark energy is the **progressive activation of branch degrees of freedom**.

As the universe evolves:
1. Medium branches saturate their current DOF
2. New DOF become accessible (crossing frequency walls)
3. Expansion accelerates as state space opens
4. Observers perceive this as "accelerating expansion"
