## String-Like Analogy (Phenomenological Only)

The results exhibit a superficial resemblance to string-theoretic imagery:
- Effectively 1D structures
- Embedded in higher-dimensional space
- Larger behavior emerging from collective organization

**However**, this is a **phenomenological analogy only**, not evidence that the model is string theory or equivalent to it. String theory involves:
- Quantum objects with specific action principles
- Extra dimensions with specific mathematical structure
- Highly constrained symmetry and consistency conditions
- Specific particle spectra

None of these have been demonstrated in QMRT.

### Correct Framing

> "Phase 7 suggests an emergent **filamentary geometric regime**: the relational network behaves approximately one-dimensionally within a higher-dimensional embedding space."

This is strong and accurate without overclaiming.
