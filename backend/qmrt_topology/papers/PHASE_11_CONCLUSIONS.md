## Executive Summary

Phase 11 tested the hypothesis that **spacetime expansion is not uniform metric expansion, but asynchronous branch development**. ## Interpretation: Staggered Branch Maturation

The data supports a **staggered branch maturation** model:

### Branch Development Sequence

```
Early Development (Low Population):
  - Nodes form and connect (Branch 1: Connectivity)
  - Loops close quickly (Branch 2: Cycles saturate to ~1.0)
  - Clustering is present but not dominant
  - Dimension rises (~0.9 → ~1.1)

Intermediate Development (Medium Population):
  - Connectivity continues growing slowly
  - Loop capacity saturates (diminishing returns)
  - Clustering begins to catch up
  - System is "Balanced" between branches
  - Dimension peaks (~1.1-1.2)

Later Development (High Population):
  - Clustering becomes competitive with loops
  - Local neighborhood densification
  - Dimension may decrease slightly (0.8-1.0)
  - System is in a different organizational regime
```

### Why This Supports "Expansion as Branch Activation"

The original hypothesis was:
> "Spacetime expansion may reflect the asynchronous growth of multiple organizational branches. As different branches mature at different rates, additional degrees of freedom become active."

The data shows:
1. **Loop branch matures first** (saturates quickly to L-C > 1)
2. **Clustering branch matures later** (only dominant at high population)
3. **Dimension is NOT tied to which branch dominates** (r = 0.036)
4. **Dimension is an early-development property** (peaks at medium population)

This suggests that **dimensional richness emerges during the early loop-dominated phase**, and the later transition to clustering represents a different kind of organizational maturation — not dimensional expansion, but perhaps **organizational consolidation**.

## Theoretical Statement

> **"In the QMRT medium, organizational development proceeds through staggered branch maturation. Loop structure (independent cycles) matures first at low population, while local clustering matures later at higher population. Effective dimension appears to be established during the early loop-dominated phase and does not increase with the later transition to clustering dominance."**

---

## What This Means for "Spacetime Expansion"

### The Original Question

Does spacetime "expand" by:
- (A) Increasing geometric radius? → **Ruled out (Phase 10)**
- (B) Increasing relational capacity? → **Partially supported**
- (C) Staggered branch activation? → **Supported**

### The Answer

Spacetime expansion in QMRT appears to be best described as **staggered branch activation** — different organizational features mature at different rates as the medium builds up.

However, **dimensional richness** appears to be established early (during loop-dominated development) and does NOT continue increasing during later organizational phases. This suggests:

1. **Dimension is a threshold property**: Once loops form, dimension "activates" to ~1.0-1.2
2. **Dimension saturates**: Further organizational development (clustering) does not increase dimension
3. **Different branches serve different functions**:
   - Loops → dimensional scaffold (how connected/space-filling)
   - Clustering → local structure consolidation (how densely organized)
