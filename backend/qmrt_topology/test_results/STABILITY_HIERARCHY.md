# Stability Hierarchy Framework

## April 2026

---

## Core Insight

**Stability is not one thing, but a hierarchy of stabilizing layers.**

Instead of asking "is it stable?" ask:
> "Which stability layers are present, and at which level?"

---

## The Five Stability Layers

| Layer | Question | Criterion |
|-------|----------|-----------|
| **1. Dynamic Persistence** | Does something continue to exist? | Activity doesn't vanish |
| **2. Structural Coherence** | Does it keep organized form? | S > 0 maintained |
| **3. Localized Persistence** | Does organization stay in one region? | S contrast > 1 |
| **4. Interactive Persistence** | Can it interact without disappearing? | Structures influence each other |
| **5. Matter-Like Stability** | Does it persist AND interact particle-like? | All layers satisfied simultaneously |

## Why This Matters

### The Model Is Not Failing Randomly

The results show systematic structure:
- Paper 3 reaches Layer 3 (localization) but not Layer 4 (interaction)
- Branch B reaches Layer 4 (interaction) but not Layer 3 (localization)
- Branch C shows Layers 3 and 4 are LINKED through τ_eq

### Matter-Like Behavior Requires Layer Alignment

> **Hypothesis: Matter requires multiple stability layers to align at once.**

Not just energy, or topology, or asymmetry — but a **stack of stabilizing effects** that reinforce one another.

---

## The Stability Ladder Test

For any future branch or model extension, ask:

1. **Does it increase lifetime?** (Layer 1)
2. **Does it preserve organization?** (Layer 2)
3. **Does it localize that organization?** (Layer 3)
4. **Does it allow interaction?** (Layer 4)
5. **Does it keep all of the above at once?** (Layer 5)

This replaces the binary "does it make matter?" with a diagnostic ladder.

## Theoretical Implications

### Why Layers Don't Automatically Align

Each layer has a different mechanism:

| Layer | Mechanism | Control Parameter |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Energy injection | Driving |
| 2 | τ-ρ coupling | β |
| 3 | Spatial β variation | β(x) |
| 4 | Phase winding | Complex field |
| 5 | ??? | Unknown |

Layer 5 requires the mechanisms to couple — which is what Branch C begins to show.

### The β-τ_eq Connection

Branch C revealed that β affects vortex lifetime through τ_eq:
$$\tau_{eq} = \frac{\tau_0}{1 + \beta \cdot \rho}$$

This links Layer 3 (localization) to vortex dynamics (Layer 4 prerequisite).

**The coupling exists** — it's just not strong enough to reach Layer 5 yet.

## Summary

### What We've Learned

1. Stability has **layers**, not a binary state
2. Current model reaches **different layers in different branches**
3. β couples to **both organization AND vortex lifetime** through τ_eq
4. **Layer 5 requires alignment** that hasn't been achieved yet

### Framework for Future Work

> Evaluate models by **which stability layers they satisfy**, not just whether they "make matter."

This is a **diagnostic framework**, not a conclusion. It organizes what we know and guides what to test next.
