## Mathematical Boundary

**The real scalar field is irrotational by construction:**

If v = ∇φ, then ∇ × v = ∇ × (∇φ) = 0 identically.

This is not a parameter issue—it's a structural limitation. No amount of β asymmetry, damping, or initial conditions can create topological defects in a single real scalar field.
